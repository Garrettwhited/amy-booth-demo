Policies & Privacy

What this work is

Amy’s sessions are spiritual guidance and personal exploration. They are not therapy, medicine, legal advice, or financial advice, and no outcomes are guaranteed. Please keep licensed professionals for clinical, medical, legal, and financial matters.

Your privacy
