The roots — healing the mother and father wounds

Staging draft — written from Amy’s own material already in her booth, for her approval. Her words replace it, in her voice, before her site goes live.

Ask Amy what her work is, and she doesn’t start with cards. She starts at the roots: “Healing the mother and father trauma and creating lasting relationships through divine intervention.”

Her teaching is that so much of what loops in a life — the patterns, the feelings that arrive from nowhere, the relationships that break the same way twice — traces back to those first two relationships. Heal at the root, and the rest of the tree stands a chance: relationships that last, and love, wealth, health, and abundance in all of its forms.

And her reminder underneath all of it: “You’re not separated from the Christ inside you. You’re a soul in a human experience.”

The way through, as she teaches it, is to build the new foundation from within. That building is the work her sittings are for.
