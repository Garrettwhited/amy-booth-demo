The Energy Mirror — Live Classes

A 4-week live class for women who keep attracting emotionally unavailable men
By Amy Lyn Bassett · Merge2Worlds

The class, in Amy’s words

  • Includes: The Energy Mirror workbook (PDF) — every student gets it before Week 1 and works in it during and between sessions
  • Format: 4 weekly group sessions, about 90 minutes each, following the workbook’s own 4-week practice plan
  • Bring: the workbook, a journal, and an open heart

The workbook comes with the class. Start free with The First Reflection — or get the full workbook there.

Week 1 — See the Mirror

Workbook: Parts 1–3, plus the Reflection Worksheet (Part 10)

  • Opening grounding: set the intention — we are not here to blame him, or ourselves. We are here to see the energy.
  • Teaching: the energy underneath him, the energy underneath you, and how his energy reflects yours back to you.
  • Guided practice in session: each woman names one pattern she can now see in the mirror.
  • Between sessions: work Parts 1–3 in the workbook. Complete one Reflection Worksheet on a moment that still stings.

Week 2 — Meet the Little Girl

Workbook: Parts 4–6, plus the Pause Practice (Part 7)

  • Opening: a short check-in — what did the mirror show you this week?
  • Teaching: where the blueprint was printed, the beliefs underneath it, and the little girl in the mirror — the younger face his energy lands on.
  • Guided practice in session: the Pause Practice — catching the inner voice that is not hers, and stepping back with love instead of stuffing it down.
  • Between sessions: work Parts 4–6. Practice the Pause daily. Write one new belief to replace an old one.

Week 3 — Balance and Release

Workbook: Parts 8–9, plus the Release Practice (Part 11)

  • Opening: a short check-in — where did you catch yourself giving from an empty current?
  • Teaching: the giving and receiving current, sealing the leaks, and balancing the two energies within you — the twin flame is already within.
  • Guided practice in session: the Release Practice — letting stored energy move out of the emotional body with love and grace.
  • Between sessions: work Parts 8–9. Seal one leak. Daily Balance Check each night, and one full Release Practice on your own.

Week 4 — Live From New Energy

Workbook: Parts 12–13 and Part 15, re-read Part 14

  • Opening: a short check-in — what feels different in your body this week?
  • Teaching: communication — the energy you speak from; intimacy — letting yourself be met; and a full session honoring the extra-sensitive women: the wisdom held in the emotional, mental, and spiritual energy bodies, and tuning in to Source energy.
  • Guided practice in session: the Body Test — learning to feel the difference between old energy and available energy before you choose.
  • Closing: each woman reads her new belief aloud. Re-read Part 14. One last Reflection Worksheet on anything that still stings — and leave with the Daily Check-In as a way of life.

What’s included

  • 4 live group sessions with Amy (about 90 minutes each)
  • The Energy Mirror workbook PDF (15 parts) — yours to keep
  • Guided practices in every session: Reflection Worksheet, Pause Practice, Release Practice, Body Test
  • Between-session workbook practices each week

Still yours to decide

  • Start date, and the day and time each week
  • Where the class is held — video call, in person, or both
  • Group size — small and intimate, or open enrollment

Those stand here the moment Amy sets them. Until then, ask about the next class.
