“Healing the mother and father trauma and creating lasting relationships through divine intervention.”

Merging Two Worlds

The Story of Amy — her memoir, in her own words.

I am a Master intuitive psychic medium and energy channel — but before I was any of those things, I was a woman living between two worlds: the world everyone could see, and the world I could feel.

I was only seven, maybe eight years old, when I first learned what depression felt like — and it wasn't even mine. It came from my mother's loneliness. A little girl isn't supposed to know a weight like that, but I could feel it. That was the beginning of my gift: before I ever had words for "medium" or "channel," I was a child who could feel what the people she loved were carrying inside.

My mother was always the one who said I was too much. Too sensitive. Too feeling. She said I thought I knew everything. But what she called "too much" was this gift with no name yet — I felt too much because I was feeling everything, including hers. And what she called "knowing everything" was a child's intuition she didn't understand.

And I believe she knew I could feel it. I believe my mother knew she carried unhealed trauma of her own — and that it was passing down to her children, the way pain travels through a family until somebody finally stops and heals it.

I started early on taking on her emotions and her feelings as if they were my own — and that became a pattern. It basically set the attachment style of every relationship that followed. In my own relationships, I carried what other people felt, and it was a struggle. When you absorb everyone else's pain, you slowly lose track of where they end and you begin.

And then there was my father.

I didn't really have an intimate relationship with my father. He worked all the time. As a kid, I was always questioning my mom: "Where's my dad?" Nobody really tells a kid why their father is absent — and I was one of those kids who asked. With my mom's depression, I developed anxiety. I remember when I was a kid, she actually took me to his work. He worked at Kalkollan Church Furniture. He did church pews, the crosses on top of the church, what not — and he wasn't even a religious man, by no means.

My father was toxic masculine — a large ego, controlling. He was the kind of man that believed the woman stayed at home, and it was not a woman's business where the man's paycheck went. He was in his late 30s when they got married, and my mom was like 16, 17. At home it was just my mother, and I could feel her resenting the marriage with my father. I didn't understand it as a kid. And it wasn't just her silence — it was outbursts. It was the silent treatment.

And it wasn't just my father. It was the archetype of all masculinity: this big ego, believing the woman should be at home with the kids, doing the chores, cooking the meals, not worrying about the bills or how much the paycheck was — and the women not really being appreciated for their femininity, and all that they ever did in the marriage. A misuse of masculine power towards a feminine energy in my ancestry. And it wasn't just about my mother — I learned the mother archetype too. I studied it. I was very observant as that young child. I saw the pattern in my siblings, too: leaving early the home to start their own families.

So I want the world to know that they need to pay attention to their children — especially now, the way the world is evolving and changing. Children develop their communication from what they live in. They come to think the way their parents communicate is the only way you get to communicate — the way they speak about money, and scarcity, lack, and all that. That was a really big thing.

That really set the stage of my own relationships. That was my type of attraction that I believed I attracted — they all kind of played out the same. I kept attracting emotionally unavailable men — the same shape as my father. The blueprint for conflict, closeness, and trust, and how parents treat each other, was something that I learned: how to love, to suffer, and how to abandon myself. And how your mother treated you — like me — taught me how to treat myself. How she talked to me became my inner voice.

What my spirit actually taught me in my latest relationship — and this goes into a twin flame journey, because I am a twin flame soul — is this: the twin flame has been put out there in so many different ways, but really, when you're on a twin flame journey, the twin flame is already within you. That's where the feminine and masculine energy became a balancing act.

And this is for awareness — for parents, or people wanting to even start a relationship with someone outside of theirself — they should really do the shadow work first, because otherwise it's already failed, because of the unconscious part of how to build the solid foundation. It can never begin solid without doing the shadow work. Now I find myself having to reflect when that voice comes in, of self-doubt, or "you're too much," or if you need to explode — reflect and take a step back, instead of stuff the stuff down without looking. It's called shadow work.

I'm not saying relationships are supposed to be perfect. There's always supposed to be some kind of growth, where you both grow and learn. But when that growth is no longer available, and the one person wants to change — the inside, and I don't mean anything changes first outside — it's a process of alchemy that happens within us. And once that starts, you can't go backwards. You won't want to go backwards.

It was about healing false insecurities that weren't mine, and learning how to put myself first — not always be the one that's the giver, but to balance the giving and receiving of love, money, whatever, affection.

And it didn't stop with me. All of the children carried it — my siblings, and then their children too. It lived in our beliefs, our habits, our habitual ways of thinking. This is just the way it is, we told ourselves. This is the way it was.

But I broke it.

How did I break it? I stopped running from what I felt. I embraced every thought, every feeling, every emotion that came to the surface — and I questioned it. Where did it come from? I traced it back, starting with my mother. And then I saw the same pattern in my own children, and it taught me. It brought me to maturity. It taught me to parent differently: no control — communication. No expectations placed on my children. Just love, spoken honestly.

Emotional trauma taught me lessons I never asked for — and healing from it taught me who I really am. I learned to balance the masculine and feminine energy within me, and that balance became my calling: helping other hearts do the same.

That is why I created Merge2Worlds. Every tarot reading, every mediumship session, every coaching conversation is about bringing your two worlds together — your pain and your power, your past and your future, the human and the spiritual.

But I won't lie to you and say the freedom was free. My children's freedom came with a very high price. Some of my children lost their own children — through relationships, in the same way the pattern always collects what it's owed. Breaking a generational pattern doesn't mean nobody bleeds. It means somebody finally says: the bleeding stops here, even if it costs me everything to say it.

And my children? They see a mother. Not a pattern. Not a price tag on love. They love me unconditionally, and they know I love them the same way — no transactions, no control. I allow them to learn their own life lessons, in their own time, and they know this: whenever they fall too hard, I will be right there by their side. That is what breaking the pattern looks like. It looks like freedom, with a mother standing close enough to catch you — and wise enough to let you fly.

But understand: this story was never really about my parents. It is about the mother I chose to become. The unhealed version of me had shadow aspects I was unconscious of — parts of me running my life from the dark, repeating what I had absorbed. My healing was bringing those shadows into conscious awareness, one by one, and reprogramming my subconscious — so that today I live the most beautiful, abundant life: led by the Christ within, guided by my higher mind.

Today I serve as a heart-centered woman leader of the new age, guiding others through healing, clarity, and emotional freedom — online and in person.

I believe all of this was God's beautiful creation — that I was the one chosen to walk the middle path: with faith, with trust, in the unknown and the unseen. It was never about believing before I could see it. It was a knowing I was born with. I always knew I had it. It's just amazing — and I want to share it with the entire world.

And understand this about me: I don't live in the past, and I'm done talking about it for its own sake. I only use it for others. I see the momentum my healing has created in my own life — and I give it back, so that you, too, can experience love, money, success, good health, and well-being. That is what Merge2Worlds is for. Two worlds became one in me. Now let me help you merge yours.

— Amy Lyn
