What a sitting with Amy actually is

Staging draft — written from Amy’s own material already in her booth, for her approval. Her words replace it, in her voice, before her site goes live.

People find Amy’s booth by playing in it — a card pulled for nothing, a ball rubbed awake, a wish put in the lamp. That play is the doorway. A sitting is what’s through the door: real time with Amy herself, where the quick read becomes the true answer.

In a sitting, Amy reads the cards and the energy in front of her and goes where it leads — the roots, the heart, the coins, whatever is actually asking. Her work, in her words, is “healing the mother and father trauma and creating lasting relationships through divine intervention.”

What she’s after is never a fortune told and forgotten. Healing, as she teaches it, is to build the new foundation from within — releasing old energy, patterns, and looping thoughts, and knowing where the feelings come from.

That’s the shape of it. The details of your sitting are yours — they start when you write your name in her book.
