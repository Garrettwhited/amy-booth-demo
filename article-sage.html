Sage & smoke — clearing, and a burn over your problem

Staging draft — written from Amy’s own material already in her booth, for her approval. Her words replace it, in her voice, before her site goes live.

Smoke is one of the oldest tools in her booth. You write the problem down — plainly, in your own words — and you watch the smoke take it, until what’s left is a clearer thought than the one you came in with. That’s the little version, and you can try it right on her table.

The bigger version is hers to do. Amy sells her sage — and when you buy one, you can have her burn it over your problem: you write the problem, and in her own time she burns that sage over it. No appointment, no sitting — your problem, her sage, her smoke.

Why smoke at all? Because some things you don’t clear alone, and because a problem you can see written down is a problem you can finally watch leave. The burning is hers. The clarity is yours to keep.
